🧮 PPC Formula
PPC = (Weight / 1000) × W × G
W = 0.85 × log₁₀(t_adj) × P
t_adj = min(15, max(1, service life))
P = Exposure Factor
G = ESG Multiplier
Processing credits reward conversion of PET into recycled products. Longer service life = more credits. Environmentally exposed products (roads, geotextiles) carry reduced exposure factors.

📌 How to Use
Step 1 — Fill the Excel template
Open your Excel workbook and fill in the manual columns for each month:
· Column A — Date
· Column B — Weight Collected / Weight Processed (kg)
· Column C — Collection Category / Product Type
· Column D — ESG Score (0–100)
Columns E–H/J are formulas and are ignored on upload.
Step 2 — Select file type & upload
Choose whether it is a Collection or Processing file, then drag & drop or click to upload. All 12 monthly sheets are read automatically.

Step 3 — Preview & Import
Review the data in the preview table, then click "Import All Entries" to load everything into the dashboard. Credits are recalculated instantly.
